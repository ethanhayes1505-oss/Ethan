# Sleep Labs: policy pages to paste into Shopify

Shopify admin → **Settings → Policies**. For each one, click it, paste the text, and **Save**.
Each section below is the full text of one policy. The text is written to match what the store does today (free US shipping, 30-day returns, 1-day processing, US + UK shipping zones).

**Please read the "Check before you paste" notes first.** They're decisions only you can make.

## Check before you paste
1. **Who pays return postage.** I wrote that the customer pays for change-of-mind returns, and that you cover it if the band arrives damaged or faulty. Change it if you want to cover all returns.
2. **Where returns go.** The text says you'll send the return address when they message you. If your supplier ships from overseas, work out your return process first.
3. **Delivery time.** I did not state a delivery window because I don't know your real one. Add it to the Shipping policy if you know it (for example "most US orders arrive in X to Y business days").
4. **UK shipping.** The store has a UK zone, so the shipping policy says UK rates show at checkout. Remove those lines if you don't want to sell to the UK.
5. **Business details.** Shopify and card networks like a legal business name and a postal address on the Contact information page. Add yours at the end.
6. This is plain-language text, not legal advice. If you're unsure, have it checked.

## Refund policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

**30-day returns**
If your SleepLabs Silent Wake Band isn't right for you, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a refund. Everyone sleeps differently, and we'd rather you send it back than keep something that doesn't work for you.

**How to start a return**
1. Message us through our contact page (/pages/contact) within 30 days of the delivery date. Include your order number.
2. We'll reply with return instructions and the address to send it to.
3. Send the band back with its charging cable, packed so it isn't damaged on the way.

**Refunds**
Once we've received and checked your return, we'll refund the price of the items to your original payment method. Your bank or card provider may take a few days to show it. Shipping protection, if you added it, is refunded only if the order is returned in full.

**Return postage**
For returns because you've changed your mind, you pay the postage to send the item back. If your band arrives damaged or faulty, contact us and we will replace it or refund you, and we'll cover the return.

**Items that arrive damaged, lost or incorrect**
If your order arrives damaged, doesn't match what you ordered, or doesn't arrive, contact us as soon as you can through the contact page with your order number and, if relevant, a photo. If you added shipping protection, it covers loss, theft or damage in transit and we will replace or refund your order.

**Questions**
Use our contact page and a real person will reply by email.

## Shipping policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

**Where we ship**
We currently ship to addresses in the United States and the United Kingdom.

**United States**
Shipping is free on every order. There is no minimum spend.

**United Kingdom**
Shipping rates for the United Kingdom are shown at checkout before you pay.

**Processing time**
Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

**Delivery time**
Delivery times depend on your address and the carrier. Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking link. You can also check your order at any time on our Track Your Order page (/apps/parcelpanel).

**Shipping protection**
At checkout you can add optional shipping protection. It covers loss, theft or damage in transit: if your order is lost, stolen or arrives damaged, we'll replace it or refund you.

**Address mistakes**
Please check your address before you place your order. If you spot a mistake, contact us straight away through the contact page and we'll do our best to fix it before the order ships. We can't be responsible for orders delivered to an address entered incorrectly at checkout.

**Customs, duties and taxes**
Prices shown at checkout include any duties and taxes that apply to your order. You won't be asked to pay extra on delivery.

**Questions**
Use our contact page and we'll get back to you by email.
