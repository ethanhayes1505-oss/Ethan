Raw findings behind `product-sheet.html`. Published sheet: https://claude.ai/artifact/7tutNjZzSK4tCu7q59nVRt

## Method
- Store pages and Shopify product data read directly (price, created date, claims).
- Live US ad counts: Meta Ad Library estimate, by page ID.
- Supplier cost: AliExpress search sorted by orders (single-unit prices; teaser prices under $1.50 ignored).
- Shipping figures are estimates, not quotes. Card fees 2.9% + $0.30. $1 = £0.76.
- Bar from the wake band test: about £40 left per order before ad spend.

## Risks found
- PureNest: EPA says ion generators do not remove gases or odours. California (CARB) requires certification for all indoor air cleaners sold there. Their page shows "June sold out (5,320 sold)" for a product created 11 July.
- Canivet: PetMD says no major vet body (AVMA, AAHA, AVDC) recommends cleaning without anaesthesia. Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences: ultrasonic toothbrush does not remove tartar in dogs. Page header says 863 reviews, review widget says 38.
- Cattasaurus: US design patent D1029404 (granted 28 May 2024).
- SmoothPaw: no vet or independent evidence found that it shortens nails; similar Walmart boxes rated 1–3 stars on small samples.
- Herding ball: "indestructible" plus free replacement guarantee; comparable Herding Buddy on Chewy rated 3.0 from 97 with punctures and zip failures; Amazon has 139 listings, 47% priced $20–$50.
- Uahpet: proprietary product, heavy, certifications needed.
- TruLink: low price; six or more golf-aid advertisers (Finalputt, Torc Swingtrainer, Golf Swing Club, Flush Daddy Golf, StriKease, Golf Improvement Hub).
