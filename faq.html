Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the DreamLabs Shock Watch — the alarm that actually gets you out of bed.

How does the DreamLabs Shock Watch wake me up? +

When your alarm goes off, the watch delivers a gentle electric pulse to your wrist combined with strong vibration. Unlike sound alarms that your brain learns to ignore, the pulse triggers an instant wake response — you're alert in seconds, not snoozing for another hour.

Does the shock hurt? +

No — it's a startle, not pain. Think of it like the static zap you get from a doorknob, but controlled and safe. You can adjust the intensity from a light tingle to a jolt strong enough to wake the deepest sleeper, so you're always in control.

I'm a really heavy sleeper. Will this actually work for me? +

Heavy sleepers are exactly who we built this for. Sound alarms fail because your brain filters out familiar noise — but it can't ignore a physical stimulus on your skin. On the highest intensity setting, combined with vibration, the Shock Watch wakes people who sleep through multiple phone alarms.

Will it wake up my partner? +

No — that's one of the best parts. The alarm is completely silent. Only you feel the pulse and vibration on your wrist, so you can wake up at 5 AM while your partner sleeps peacefully.

How long does the battery last? +

A full charge lasts 5–10 days depending on how often the alarm goes off and your intensity settings. It recharges quickly with the included USB charger, so it's always ready for the morning.

Is it comfortable to sleep in? +

Yes — the band is made from soft, skin-friendly silicone that's flexible, breathable, and gentle on your wrist all night. The watch itself is lightweight and low-profile, so most users forget they're even wearing it.

What if it doesn't work for me? Do you offer refunds? +

We stand behind the Shock Watch with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If it doesn't transform your mornings, return it for a full refund — no interrogation, no hoops.
