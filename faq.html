Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the DreamLabs Shock Watch — the alarm that actually gets you out of bed.

How does the DreamLabs Shock Watch wake me up? +

When your alarm goes off, the watch delivers a gentle electric pulse to your wrist combined with strong vibration. Unlike sound alarms that your brain learns to ignore, the pulse triggers an instant wake response — you're alert in seconds, not snoozing for another hour.

Does the shock hurt? +

No — it's a startle, not pain. Think of it like the static zap you get from a doorknob, but controlled and safe. You can adjust the intensity across multiple levels, from a light tingle to a jolt strong enough to wake the deepest sleeper. Most users start low and find their perfect setting within a few nights.

Is it safe to use every day? +

Yes. The pulse is a low-voltage, low-current stimulus similar to TENS devices used in physiotherapy, and it only lasts a fraction of a second. It's safe for daily use on healthy adults. Note: if you have a pacemaker, implanted medical device, epilepsy, or heart condition, or if you are pregnant, consult your doctor before use.

I'm a really heavy sleeper. Will this actually work for me? +

Heavy sleepers are exactly who we built this for. Sound alarms fail because your brain filters out familiar noise — but it can't ignore a physical stimulus on your skin. On the highest intensity setting, combined with vibration, the Shock Watch has woken users who sleep through multiple phone alarms, sirens, and even fire drills.

Will it wake up my partner? +

No — that's one of the best parts. The alarm is completely silent. Only you feel the pulse and vibration on your wrist, so you can wake up at 5 AM while your partner sleeps peacefully. Perfect for couples with different schedules, shift workers, and early-morning gym sessions.

Can I turn off the snooze, so I can't cheat? +

Yes. You can disable snooze entirely, or enable escalation mode — every time you snooze, the next pulse gets stronger. There's no "just five more minutes" when your wrist disagrees.

How long does the battery last? +

A full charge lasts up to 7 days of normal use, and charging takes about 90 minutes via the included magnetic USB charger. The watch warns you well in advance when the battery is low, so it never dies the night before an important morning.

Is it waterproof? +

The Shock Watch is IP67 water-resistant — sweat, rain, and hand-washing are no problem. We recommend taking it off for swimming and showers to maximize its lifespan.

Will it fit my wrist? Is it comfortable to sleep in? +

The adjustable strap fits wrists from 140mm to 210mm (about 5.5" to 8.3"). The body is lightweight and low-profile with a soft silicone band designed specifically for overnight wear — most users forget they have it on.

How do I set alarms? Do I need an app? +

You can set alarms directly on the watch, or use the free DreamLabs app (iOS & Android) to schedule multiple alarms, customize intensity, set different alarms for different days, and track your wake-up streaks. The watch works fully offline once your alarms are set — no phone needed at night.

Orders are processed within 24–48 hours. Standard shipping takes 7–12 business days depending on your location, and you'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

We stand behind the Shock Watch with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If it doesn't transform your mornings, return it for a full refund — no interrogation, no hoops. Every watch also comes with a 12-month warranty covering manufacturing defects.
