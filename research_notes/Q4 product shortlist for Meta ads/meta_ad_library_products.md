# Meta Ad Library: physical products advertised to US shoppers by DTC / dropshipping-style stores (checked 23 Nov 2026)

**How to read this file.** Every fact is tagged **VERIFIED** (seen in a Meta Ad Library search result on 23 Nov 2026: page name, ad headline, ad start date, active-ad count) or **UNVERIFIED** (from a blog/roundup search snippet, or my own inference). Ad Library links are the public snapshot URLs returned by the tool. Dates are UTC.

**Hard limits of this research (read before trusting anything below):**
- The Ad Library tool returned only: page name, ad headline (link title), ad creation time, delivery start time, snapshot URL, currency, and an *estimated* count of matching active ads. It did **not** return ad body text, the landing-page URL, spend, impressions, or reach. So "angle/hook" below comes from headlines only.
- Results came back **newest first, max 50 per call**. Because nearly every store relaunched creatives for Black Friday between 19 and 23 Nov, the sample is dominated by ads that started in the last 5 days. **I could almost never see an advertiser's oldest active ad**, so "running 30+ days" is verified for only one advertiser (Shopekoh, resistance bands). Absence of an old start date here does NOT mean the product is new.
- Keyword matching is loose (not exact-phrase; quoting the phrase made no difference). Queries with generic words ("neck reading light", "sunrise alarm clock", "phone lock box", "page turner remote kindle") returned mostly novel/drama-app ads, so the counts for those are meaningless and those products are UNVERIFIED in the Ad Library.
- **Every store/blog page fetch failed** (network allow-list: copyfy.io, sellthetrend.com, tradelle.io, cjdropshipping.com all unresolvable). I therefore could **not** open any store product page. **Prices are VERIFIED only where the price is printed in the ad headline** (3 cases). All other prices are missing, not guessed.
- Weight and "sourceable from Alibaba/AliExpress" judgements are my inference (UNVERIFIED) in every case; no supplier listing was opened.
- No ad account was touched; only Ad Library search was used.
