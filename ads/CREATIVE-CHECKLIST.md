# SleepLabs ad creative checklist

Every ad gets checked against this list **before** it is shown. If any item fails, fix it first.

## Lessons learned (Angle 1, Ads 1 and 2: binned)
- A pretty lifestyle photo with a tiny product in it wastes money. People scroll in about a second, and if they can't see what's being sold, they don't stop.
- A fake "Shop now" button and a floating product card made the ad look like a website banner and felt dropshippy. Meta already shows a real, clickable CTA button under every ad.
- A nice headline alone doesn't say what the product is.
- I knew all of this and still built it. The checks below come **before** an ad is shown, not after.

## Before showing any ad
1. **Product is the hero.** The band is sharp and takes up at least about a quarter of the frame, and it matches the real product (graphite ribbed strap, white LED digits running along the band).
2. **One-second test.** Shrink the ad to phone-feed size (about 400px wide). Can you tell straight away that it's a wristband alarm? If not, redo it.
3. **Say what it is.** One plain line on the image, e.g. "The vibrating alarm only the wearer feels."
4. **No fake UI.** No fake buttons, no floating cards, no stickers, no "% OFF" badges. The CTA lives in Meta's own button and the ad text.
5. **Maximum 3 text items:** headline, what-it-is line, brand/price. Lots of empty space.
6. **Perceived value.** Macro detail, premium light, real textures (skin, linen, silicone). It should look like a $100+ brand, not a $15 gadget.
7. **AI artifact check at full size.** Crop and inspect the hands, faces, the product, any text or digits, and objects. No warped fingers, fake writing or garbled numbers.
8. **Honest claims only.** Nothing implying the buzz is silent to others until the motor has been tested in a quiet room. No fake prices, no health claims, and no "you have [condition]" lines.
9. **Story still reads.** The partner/couple angle should still come across, but secondary to the product.
10. **Every new ad must look different.** A second ad needs a different concept or format (e.g. photo vs text-led vs comparison vs product still life), not the same shot with a new headline. Meta treats near-identical images as one creative. (Lesson from Angle 1 Ad 2, binned for looking like Ad 1.)
11. **Sizes.** 4:5 (1080x1350) for feed. Plan safe zones for 9:16 (keep text out of the top ~14% and bottom ~20%).
