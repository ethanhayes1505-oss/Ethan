## Who it's for and why
- **Buyer:** the considerate early riser, who's also the person wearing the band. Gym-goers, early shifts, 5am starters. Men over-index: 20% of men vs 10% of women already use a silent alarm for a partner (AASM 2025, verified against the PDF), peaking at ages 25–44.
- **Customer language:**
  - "without having to wake up my wife and dogs" (Hacker News)
  - "How to wake up early without waking everyone" (TikTok titles; the Meta headline uses this almost word for word)
  - "loud to me but quiet to everyone else in the house" (X)
- **Competitors:** Velore ran "Wake Up Without Waking Them" as 9 ads and has stopped. FitSleeps tested an "early-shift" headline. No premium brand owns this angle on Meta right now.
- **Creative:** different from both Angle 1 ads. It's out of bed in the hallway, with a sportswear-campaign feel, and the band is the hero.

## Before you spend
- "She sleeps through all of it" and "the rest of the house doesn't" claim the buzz isn't heard. Do the quiet-bedroom test first.
- The first-person lines are a scripted ad voice, not a review. No stars or customer names.
