## Why this one
- **Format:** a comparison (old way vs new way). It's one of the strongest-converting static formats for a product like this, because it explains the product and answers "why not just use my phone?" in one glance. It's also a different format from all three live ads.
- **Customer language:** the captions come from "loud to me but quiet to everyone else in the house" (X), simplified to heard vs felt.
- **Premium finish:** matching photography on both sides, brand serif type, no stickers or badges.

## Before you spend
- "Felt by you only" and "sleeps on" claim the partner isn't disturbed. Do the quiet-bedroom test first.
- The left panel is a staged scene, not a customer.
