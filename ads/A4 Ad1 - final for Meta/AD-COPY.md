## Why this one
- **A new format: first-person POV.** You're looking down over your own wrist into the crib, which none of the other six ads does. The palette is new too: sage-grey nursery and one warm night-light.
- **Product first, reading the right way.** Because the arm points away from the camera, the band runs across the frame and 04:45 reads exactly as the wearer would see it. The crop puts the band at over a quarter of the frame width.
- **Customer language:** the headline comes from the research hook "Took an hour to get the baby down. My alarm isn't undoing that." The Meta headline is the research's 4A line, from the Mumsnet poster "04:45… don't want to wake up DP or DS".
- **Nobody else is running it.** No vibrating-alarm brand showed up running a baby or parent angle on Meta. Homedics stocks its WakeBand at buybuy BABY under "Baby Sleep Soothers", so parents are already buying in this category.

## Before you spend
- "The house stays asleep, baby included" and "won't undo it" claim the vibration doesn't wake anyone else. Do the quiet-room test first, ideally in an actual nursery with the door open.
- Make no safety or health claims about babies or sleep, such as "safe for baby" or "better sleep for your child". The ad only says the alarm doesn't ring.
- The baby and parent are a staged, AI-generated scene, not a customer.
