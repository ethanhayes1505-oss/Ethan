## Why this one
- **Hook source:** "Backup alarm" is the customers' own phrase. "Done being her backup" got 300+ Reddit replies, and the Threads post "the warm-up alarm isn't for me" is the same feeling.
- **Proven structure, not copied wording:** this is HiStrips' strongest couples ad structure. Their "Stop Being Their Alarm Clock" ran as a 14-ad batch. We keep the structure, not their words.
- **The snooze chain:** the 5:30 / 5:39 / 5:48 line is the exact trigger moment people describe, a partner who snoozes every few minutes.
- **A real test against Ad 1:** the visual is different (her hand resting on his wrist), so this tests a new hook and image rather than a near-copy of Ad 1.

## Before you spend
- "I sleep through it" and the image both say she isn't woken. Do the quiet-bedroom test first. If the buzz is audible across the bed, change that line.
- "Snooze" in the copy describes his old phone alarm. Don't claim the band has a snooze button unless it does.
- The first-person lines are a scripted ad voice, not a customer review. Don't add stars or a customer name.
