## Why this hook
- The opening line is the woken partner talking. That's the one angle no vibrating-alarm brand is running as its main ad on Meta.
- It's backed by the strongest pattern in the competitor research:
  - HiStrips relaunched "Stop Being Their Alarm Clock" in a 14-ad batch.
  - Velore ran "I don't even hear him leave anymore."
  - Mumsnet threads like "His f**king alarm !!!" show how strongly people feel about it.
- It describes the situation, not a flaw in the viewer, so it stays inside Meta's personal-attributes rule.

## Before you spend
- The image and the copy both say she isn't woken. Test the band in a quiet bedroom first. If the buzz is audible across the bed, change the headline and the "I don't" line.
- Everything else in the copy comes from your own product page: no app, set on the band, soft adjustable strap, USB charging, $39.99 / $64.99 2-pack, 30-day returns. Keep them matching if the page changes.
- The first-person lines are a scripted ad voice, not a customer review. Don't add stars or a customer name to them.
