## Why this one
- **Different from Ad 1.** Ad 1 is an emotional lifestyle photo. This is text-led, with a product still life and no people, so Meta treats it as a separate creative and you learn which style wins.
- **The hook is the trigger moment from the research.** A partner snoozes every few minutes from 5:30. People call themselves the "backup alarm", e.g. "done being her backup" (300+ Reddit replies).
- **The structure comes from HiStrips' best couples batch** ("Stop Being Their Alarm Clock", 14 ads), rewritten in customers' words.

## Before you spend
- "I stay asleep" and the headline claim she isn't woken. Do the quiet-bedroom test first.
- "Snooze" describes his old phone alarm. Don't say the band has snooze unless it does.
- The first-person lines are a scripted ad voice, not a review. No stars or customer names.
