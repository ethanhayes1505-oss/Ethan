## Why this one
- **Different from all four live ads.** They're all dark, blue-hour and night-time. This one is bright, warm and daytime, shot like a gift catalogue. It's a product still life with no people, and the offer is the hero.
- **Product first.** Both displays face the camera straight on and read 05:30, like a watch catalogue shot.
- **Competitors right now (Ad Library, Nov 6–7):** Loftie launched 15 "🎁 Early Bird Holiday Sale | 15% OFF" ads and "FREE Night Lights With Every Clock". FitSleeps relaunched "54% OFF + 100-Day Trial" in a big batch. Rise Bands is running "THE PERFECT GIFT FOR SOMEONE DEAF 🎁", a personal-attribute line Meta can reject. None of them sells a couples 2-pack. This ad takes that gap, with a real price and no fake "% OFF".
- **Customer language:** "Different alarm times, same bed" and "give them an alarm only they can feel" come from the gift research (the Mumsnet poster who bought her husband a vibrating watch "so he could ditch using his iphone as an alarm and waking me up"). It's also the frustration in titles like "3 Reasons Not to Murder Your Partner Even Though Their Alarm Has Gone Off 12 Times in the Last Hour".

## Before you spend
- **The gift box and cushions in the photo are styling, not your real packaging.** If buyers expect to unbox this and get a polybag, you'll get complaints and returns. Either match it (a cream box plus tissue is cheap) or say "gift-ready packaging" only once it's true.
- "Only the wearer feels the buzz" and "the other side of the bed stays asleep" claim the partner isn't woken. Do the quiet-bedroom test first.
- Both bands show the same time, because they're both clocks. Don't add a line saying each band holds a different alarm unless you've confirmed it does (it should, if each band is set separately).
- Only add "Order by [date] for Christmas" once your carrier has confirmed the cutoff.
