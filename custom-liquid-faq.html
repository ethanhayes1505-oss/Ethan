Your questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you start.

Does red light therapy actually work for eye bags and dark circles?

Red light therapy is one of the most researched at-home skincare treatments. Red light (around 630–660nm) is absorbed by your skin cells, where it helps them produce more energy and more collagen, the protein that keeps skin firm and smooth.

In a 2023 clinical trial of 137 women, 10 sessions of red light over 4 weeks reduced wrinkle volume around the eyes by around 30%. In a separate study, people using LED light under the eyes reported less puffiness and lighter-looking dark circles after 6 weeks.

It works best on puffiness, thin or crepey skin, fine lines and tired-looking dark circles. If your bags are caused by fat pushing forward under the eye (often genetic and still there after a full night’s sleep), red light can improve the skin over them but won’t remove them completely.

Is it safe for my eyes?

Yes, when used as directed. Red and near-infrared light contain no UV, so they don’t carry the risks of sunlight or sunbeds, and the light won’t burn your skin.

The LEDs are bright, so keep your eyes closed during each session and never stare directly into the light. If you have an eye condition, have had recent eye surgery or take medication that makes you sensitive to light, check with your doctor or optician first.

When will I see results?

Red light works gradually as your skin builds new collagen, so using it regularly matters more than long sessions.

Some people notice less puffiness and fresher-looking eyes within the first 2 weeks. Smoother skin and softer fine lines usually show from around 4 weeks, with the best results after 8–12 weeks of regular use.

Tip: take a photo in the same lighting before you start, so you can compare.

How do I use it?

1. Start with clean, dry skin and remove any eye makeup.

2. Position the device under your eyes and close your eyes.

3. Switch it on for one 10-minute session. It turns off automatically.

4. Apply your eye cream or serum afterwards.

Use it 3–5 times a week for the first 8–12 weeks, then 2–3 times a week to keep your results.
