The Big Rules

Offside

You must have two opponents (usually the keeper + a defender) between you and the goal line when a team-mate passes to you. Stand behind the last defender — if you're level, you're onside.

Handball

No touching the ball with your hand or arm — on purpose, or by making your body bigger. Only the goalkeeper can handle it, and only inside their own penalty area.

Cards

The referee shows cards for unfair play.

Yellow = warningRed = you're off!

Fouls

No tripping, pushing, kicking, holding or dangerous tackles. The other team gets a free kick — or a penalty if the foul happened in the box.

Ball Out Of Play — How It Restarts

Over the side line? Throw-in (both hands, over your head, both feet on the ground).  •  Over the goal line off an attacker? Goal kick.  •  Over the goal line off a defender? Corner kick.  •  Whole ball must cross the whole line — on the line is still in!

The Unwritten Rules

Nobody writes these down…

…but every real footballer follows them. This is what makes you a player people want on their team.
  • Kick the ball out if an opponent is injured.
  • Give it straight back to them at the throw-in.
  • Shake hands with every opponent at the end.
  • Never argue with the referee — they decide, you play.
  • Clap your keeper when they make a mistake, not just a save.
  • Don't over-celebrate against a team you're thrashing.
Now go and play! Rules set by The Football Association (The FA) & IFAB — Laws of the Game
