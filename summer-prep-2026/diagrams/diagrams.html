Passer

Challenge: how many different gates can your pair pass through in 90 seconds? You cannot use the same gate twice in a row.

Diagram 3 — Conditioned Game: 4 v 4 to Target Zones

30 × 20 m · 3 pitches running at once · 15 minutes
Green team (attacking left zone)
Red team (attacking right zone)
Ball  — solid = pass, dashed = run
Score: 1 point for dribbling the ball under control into the target zone and stopping it.
Condition: your team must complete 3 passes before you can score.
Bonus: 2 points if the ball reaches the zone without the other team touching it.
