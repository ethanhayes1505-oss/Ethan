# Ethan

## Hero — Landing (Shopify section)

A high-converting hero section for the top of the home page: white background,
product image as the centerpiece, a bold **Shop Now** button in the middle of the
image, headline + subheadline above and trust badges below. Fully editable from
the Shopify theme customizer.

- `sections/hero-landing.liquid` — the Shopify section
- `hero-preview.html` — open in a browser to preview the layout (uses a grey
  placeholder where your product photo goes)

### How to add it to your store

1. In Shopify admin go to **Online Store → Themes → ⋯ → Edit code**.
2. Under **Sections**, click **Add a new section**, name it `hero-landing`,
   and paste in the contents of `sections/hero-landing.liquid`.
3. Open the theme **Customizer**, go to your home page, remove the old hero and
   click **Add section → Hero — Landing**.
4. Click the section and upload your product photo (the 4-watches image),
   then tweak the heading, button text/link, badges and colors as you like.

Tips for the image: use the white-background version of your product photo so it
blends into the page, ideally at least 1600px wide.
